How to Calculate Your Return on Investment for SEO

Search Engine Optimization is the highest-ROI digital acquisition channel because organic search clicks carry zero marginal cost per click. Unlike Google Ads or Facebook Ads where traffic immediately stops when you pause ad spend, organic rankings continue generating qualified buyer inquiries month after month.

The mathematical formula used by enterprise growth leaders to evaluate SEO is:

Monthly SEO Revenue = (Search Volume × SERP CTR) × Website Conversion Rate × Sales Close Rate × Customer Lifetime Value

By optimizing technical Core Web Vitals, building high-authority local citations, deploying Schema.org 2.0 entity graphs, and implementing sub-3-second WhatsApp routing funnels, you can predictably capture top-3 rankings and scale your bottom line.
