How to Calculate Your Return on Investment for SEO

Search Engine Optimization is the highest-ROI digital acquisition channel because organic search clicks carry zero marginal cost per click. Unlike Google Ads, organic rankings continue generating qualified buyer inquiries month after month.

The formula used by enterprise growth leaders to evaluate SEO:

Monthly SEO Revenue = (Search Volume × SERP CTR) × Conversion Rate × Close Rate × Deal Value

By optimizing technical Core Web Vitals, building high-authority local citations, and deploying Schema.org entity graphs, you can predictably capture top-3 rankings and scale your bottom line.
